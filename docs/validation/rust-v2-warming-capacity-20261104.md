# Adaptive warming capacity validation

Qualified source: `e05d42c6`. The strict warming article window now follows spare
capacity at the first available provider priority level. It targets a quarter of
effective connection capacity as reserve and caps the window at 24. The advanced
floor defaults to two and accepts one through eight; on small pools the floor can
exceed the reserve. Foreground/global transfer pressure reduces new scheduling to
the floor. Already dispatched responses drain normally and remain counted until
physical completion. Foreground waits still interrupt the enclosing warm block.

The requested range bounds article scheduling. Warming retains strict revision,
CRC and final-media checks, separate workload/connection lanes, decoded-memory
reuse, daily fill admission and durable response-byte credits. This is one warm
job lane with adaptive article concurrency, not parallel full-file jobs.

Parallel warming exposed an existing response-credit stall. A connection could
retain its unfinished refill future after another connection refunded enough
credits to proceed. That future could hold the shared refill mutex while the
connection became idle. Usable credits now cancel that stale refill; finishing a
response also clears the connection's refill state before idle/keepalive.

`connections_per_job` is advanced-only. It does not belong on the setup wizard's
critical path and does not change the wizard version. Environment-owned values
use the existing managed settings path. Typed legacy policy migration carries
`ConnectionsPerJob` over with warming still disabled pending activation review.
